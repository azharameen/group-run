# Drafting Skill

## Objectives
1. Convert raw idea descriptions into formal Siemens Patent Disclosure documents.
2. Structure the document into standard sections: Title, Field of Invention, Background, Summary, Detailed Description, and Claims Outline.
3. Incorporate prior-art differentiators explicitly into the detailed description.

## Guidelines
- Use precise engineering and patent terminology.
- Provide clear reference to system components, data flows, and logical steps.
- Ensure all technical claims are properly supported in the detailed description.
