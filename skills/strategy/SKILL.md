# Siemens Strategy Alignment

Validate how well an invention aligns with Siemens strategic technology areas including Digital Industries, Smart Infrastructure, Mobility, Healthcare, and Financial Services.

## Evaluation

1. Identify the primary Siemens business unit that would own this IP
2. Check for portfolio conflicts with known Siemens patent families
3. Assess Siemens-specific competitive advantage
4. Estimate Technology Readiness Level (1-9)
5. Recommend next steps aligned with Siemens innovation strategy
