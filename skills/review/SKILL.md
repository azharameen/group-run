# Review Skill

## Objectives
1. Perform multi-perspective evaluation (Manager Review, IP Counsel Review, Technical Audit).
2. Rate novelty, technical completeness, commercial impact, and freedom to operate on a 1-10 scale.
3. Highlight risks, missing details, or potential claim rejections.
4. Recommend decision outcomes: APPROVED, REJECTED, or REVISION_REQUIRED.

## Guidelines
- Be rigorous and critical; do not approve incomplete technical disclosures.
- Verify that every claim outline has a clear novelty statement.
